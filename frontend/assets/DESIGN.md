## Brand & Style
This design system embodies a serene, editorial, and contemporary approach to travel discovery and itinerary management. It blends Scandinavian minimalism with botanical, earthy undertones, counterbalanced by a tech-forward night mode built for low-light expedition planning. 

The aesthetic is grounded in calm utility: spacious compositions, restrained ornamentation, sharp typographic hierarchy, and purposeful chromatic pacing. It avoids cliché decorative gradients or skeuomorphic travel cues, favoring structured content blocks, pristine micro-interactions, and high-legibility data displays.

## Colors
The palette operates with strict chromatic intentionality:

### Light Theme
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAF7`)**: Airy, ultra-clean off-white background preventing harsh optical glare.
- **Surface Layering (`#E1F0DA`, `#D4E7C5`, `#BFD8AF`)**: Progressive atmospheric tiers for navigation bars, card containers, and nested widgets.
- **Brand Ink & Primary Element (`#2D5A27`, `#1C2D1F`)**: Deep forest pine for actionable key components, primary text, and high-contrast structural iconography.
- **Muted Green Accent (`#99BC85`)**: Supporting interactive accents, secondary buttons, active indicator pills, and subtle track backgrounds.

### Dark Theme Mode
- **Backdrop & Surfaces (`#0B110D`, `#121A14`, `#18241C`)**: Deep obsidian-slate foundations offering high atmospheric contrast without pure-black harshness.
- **Signal Accents (`#A3E635`, `#BEF264`, `#4ADE80`)**: High-visibility electric lime and luminous pistachio utilized sparingly for critical CTAs, active itinerary pins, metrics, and flight statuses.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances contemporary editorial warmth with utilitarian legibility:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** governs headings, narrative titles, and editorial travel descriptions, lending a soft, humanistic, yet architectural presence.
- **Inter** provides dense, ultra-crisp execution for operational dashboard data, itinerary timelines, flight manifests, metadata chips, and form controls.
- Headlines maintain negative letter-spacing for compactness, while metadata labels use uppercase tracking for scannability.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, condensing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with 24px gutters and 32px to 48px outer canvas margins. Multi-pane dashboard architecture (fixed contextual navigation sidebar, fluid itinerary canvas, and sticky summary utility rails).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Collapses sidebar to an icon dock; itinerary planning widgets stack vertically.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. Bottom app-bar navigation, full-width edge-to-edge modular cards, and horizontal swiping itineraries.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons deep drop shadows in favor of tonal surface stepping and low-contrast borders:
- **Tonal Stepping (Light Mode)**: Depth is achieved by placing cards (`#FFFFFF` or `#E1F0DA`) over the off-white canvas (`#F8FAF7`), complemented by micro-borders (`1px solid rgba(28, 45, 31, 0.08)`).
- **Tonal Stepping (Dark Mode)**: Cards rely on layered slate (`#121A14`, `#18241C`) resting over `#0B110D`, framed by hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Focus Glows**: Key floating elements (quick action menus, itinerary add triggers) receive subtle diffuse colored shadows (`0 8px 24px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.12)` in light mode; `0 8px 30px rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.18)` in dark mode).

## Shapes
The shape language combines structured modern cards with organic, pill-shaped action surfaces:
- **Cards, Panels & Containers**: Formed with 16px corner radii (`rounded-lg`), delivering a soft, non-aggressive architectural container.
- **Buttons, Tags & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` border radius) to emphasize quick touch targets and create contrast against rectangular layout grids.
- **Inputs & Dropdowns**: 8px corner radii (`rounded`) for dependable, functional entry fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#2D5A27` background with white text in light mode; `#A3E635` with `#0B110D` text in dark mode. Height: 44px (desktop), 48px (mobile). Padding: 0 24px.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Semi-transparent surface fill (`#E1F0DA` in light mode, `#18241C` in dark mode) with high-contrast text and a subtle 1px border.
- **Interactive State**: Micro-scale transition (`scale(0.98)`) on active tap; subtle ambient glow on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills (Transit, Weather, Booking)**: Pill-shaped, minimal height (24px - 28px), uppercase typography (`label-caps`). Light mode uses `#E1F0DA` fill with `#1C2D1F` ink; dark mode utilizes `#18241C` base with vibrant accent text (`#BEF264` or `#4ADE80`).

### Cards & Itinerary Modules
- **Destination Guide Cards**: White or `#E1F0DA` base with 16px radius, featuring high-aspect-ratio travel imagery trimmed to 12px inner radius, accompanied by deep pine metadata tags.
- **Timeline / Day Planners**: Left-aligned chronological step indicator connecting nodes via a 2px vertical rule (`#D4E7C5` in light, `#18241C` in dark). Interactive cards shift horizontally by 4px on hover.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Search & Filter Bars**: Pill-shaped or 12px rounded enclosure with `#FFFFFF` background (light) or `#121A14` (dark), surrounded by a 1px border. Focus states transition the border to primary pine (`#2D5A27`) or electric lime (`#A3E635`).

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Checkboxes**: 18px rounded squares (4px radius). Checked state displays full primary color fill with an off-white or dark obsidian checkmark icon.
- **Toggles**: Pill tracks housing a smooth circular sliding thumb, giving tactile feedback during mode shifts (e.g., Light/Dark or Currency toggles).

### Travel Stats & Widgets
- **Metric Cards**: Clean numeric counters using `headline-lg` paired with `label-caps` subtitles. Minimalist circular progress rings rendered in `#99BC85` or `#4ADE80` tracks without background visual clutter.
